Privacy Policy — Moving Day Chaos

Last updated: 2026-11-23

Moving Day Chaos ("the game") is made by Karam (karmontal). This policy explains what the game does with data.

What the game stores. Your progress (stars, money, upgrades) and settings are saved only on your device. They are not sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Online play. When you host or join an online game, the game connects to our connection server so players can find each other with a room code. To do that, your device's IP address and a temporary room code pass through the server, and game traffic may be relayed through it if a direct connection fails. We do not store this information after the session ends, and we do not use it for anything else. LAN play stays on your local network.

What the game does not do. No accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics or tracking, no access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone. We do not sell or share data.

Children. The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
